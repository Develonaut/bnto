## Who We're Actually Competing With

The honest answer is not Zapier. Not on day one. Not even close.

The people we want — designers, small teams, solo operators — aren't using Zapier. They're Googling "compress images online" and bookmarking TinyPNG. They're converting a file on CloudConvert and never coming back. They're doing one thing, on one site, with no memory, no reuse, and no connection between any of it.

**That's the gap Bnto walks into.** Not workflow automation for enterprises. The fragmented world of single-purpose tools that small teams cobble together for everyday tasks.

---

## The Real Competitors: Single-Purpose Tools

### TinyPNG
**What they do:** Compress PNG and JPEG images in the browser
**Why people use it:** Dead simple. Drag, drop, download. It works.
**Where they fall short:**
- 20 images/month free, then $25/year
- 5MB file size limit
- Compress only — can't resize, rename, convert, or chain anything
- No memory — every visit starts from scratch
- One job, forever

**Our advantage:** Bnto does everything TinyPNG does plus resize, convert, rename — in a single flow. No limits. No $25/year. And you can save it and run it again.

### Squoosh (Google)
**What they do:** Browser-based image compression with fine-grained control
**Why people use it:** Free, private (runs in-browser), modern formats (WebP, AVIF)
**Where they fall short:**
- One image at a time — no batch processing on the web UI
- No saving, no reuse, no flow
- Great for precision, terrible for "I have 50 images to process"

**Our advantage:** Batch from day one. Save the flow. Run it again next week.

### CloudConvert
**What they do:** Convert files between 200+ formats
**Why people use it:** Handles almost any format combination
**Where they fall short:**
- Pay per conversion after a small free allotment
- Vendor-hosted, internet required
- No chaining — convert is all it does
- Pricing scales against you as you use it more

**Our advantage:** Convert is a node in a bnto flow, not the whole product. Chain it with compress, resize, rename — all in one run, no per-conversion billing.

### The Pattern Across All of Them

Every single-purpose tool shares the same failure mode: **they solve one problem and stop there.** You need 4 bookmarks to do what one bnto flow handles. None of them remember you. None of them compose. None of them grow with you.

And critically — they all start metering you the moment you become a real user. TinyPNG caps at 20 images free. CloudConvert charges per conversion. The people who use these tools most are the ones being penalized most.

## The Automation Platform Competitors (Future Reference)

These aren't our day-one competition but they're who we'll be compared to as we grow.

### Zapier
**Pricing:** Free (5 Zaps, 100 tasks/mo), Starter $29.99/mo, Pro $73.50/mo
**Built for:** Enterprises connecting SaaS tools
**Why small teams bounce:** Per-task pricing. No local execution. Can't process files or images.

### Make (formerly Integromat)
**Pricing:** Free (1,000 ops/mo), Core $10.59/mo, Pro $18.82/mo
**Why small teams bounce:** Still operation-based pricing. Complex UI. No local execution.

### n8n
**Pricing:** Self-hosted free (requires Docker), Cloud from EUR 24/mo
**Why small teams bounce:** Self-hosting requires Docker + a server. Fair-code license (not truly open). Overkill for simple tasks.
**Our advantage:** MIT licensed, single binary, desktop app, no Docker, simpler format
